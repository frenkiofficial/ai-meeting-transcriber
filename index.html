    How it works

    1

    Paste the transcript

    From any meeting tool — Zoom, Meet, Teams, or your own notes. One speaker per line, “Name: …” format works best.

    2

    We read it like a human would

    Key points are scored, decision language is spotted, and commitments are matched to the person who made them.

    3

    Share the notes

    Copy any section, or download the whole thing as .txt or .md and drop it straight into Slack, Notion, or email.

    Good to know

    Do I need to upload audio?

    No. This demo works from a text transcript. In the full version, audio is transcribed first, then summarized the same way.

    How are due dates found?

    Dates mentioned next to a commitment (“by Friday”, “by Dec 2”) are pulled out automatically. Anything vague is marked “No date set”.

    What if a line has no speaker?

    It still counts. Lines without a “Name:” prefix are grouped under the previous speaker, or “Unattributed” if there is none.

    Is my transcript stored anywhere?

    No. Everything in this demo runs locally in your browser. Close the tab and it is gone.
